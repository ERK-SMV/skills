# Référentiel National Qualité (QUALIOPI) — condensed for Odoo training design

Source: Guide de lecture, Référentiel national Qualité (art. L.6316-3 du code du travail), V.9 – 8 janvier 2024, Ministère du Travail / DGEFP. This is a condensed working reference, not a legal substitute — re-check the source guide (`guide_qualiopi_0.pdf`, kept alongside this skill's originating conversation) before an actual audit.

## How to use this file

For every training built with this skill, walk the **7 critères** below and make sure the artifacts the skill produces (program sheet, prerequisite questionnaire, convention, évaluations) cover the indicators that apply. Don't try to satisfy indicators that don't apply to your situation (see **Sous-traitance note** below) — over-engineering paperwork for indicators nobody will audit wastes the trainer's time.

**Sous-traitance note:** most indicators carry a specific clause for organismes acting as sous-traitant of another training body. If the Odoo trainer delivers under Digital League's Academy (or another already-certified OF) rather than under its own Qualiopi certification, several indicators (info diffusion, results indicators, financer feedback, etc.) become the *donneur d'ordres'* responsibility, not the trainer's — check each indicator's "Sous-traitance" line before building an artifact for it. Confirm with the user/Digital League which contractual role applies (direct OF vs. sous-traitant) before assuming full-scope compliance is needed.

**Non-conformité severity:** indicators not listed below default to "non-respect (même partiel) → non-conformité majeure". Indicators **1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 12, 13, 17, 18, 19, 23, 24, 25, 28, 30** can instead receive a graded *mineure* non-conformity for partial compliance.

**Nouveaux entrants** (first year as OF, or first year on a new action category): indicators **2, 3, 11, 13, 14, 19, 22, 24, 25, 26, 32** only require the *process to be formalized* at the initial audit — actual implementation is checked at the surveillance audit a year later. Useful to know if the Odoo training activity is brand new.

## Glossary (selected terms used above)

- **Bénéficiaire**: the person receiving the training.
- **Prérequis**: prerequisites needed before a training; absence of prerequisites must be stated explicitly, not left blank.
- **Positionnement**: the process of situating the learner against the training's objectives, to adapt content — can be as light as checking diplomas/experience if justified.
- **Objectifs opérationnels et évaluables**: objectives stated so they can actually be measured, not vague ambitions.
- **Évaluation à chaud**: immediate post-training feedback (satisfaction, perceived acquisition).
- **Évaluation à froid**: delayed feedback (weeks/months later) checking real-world application.
- **Aléa / Difficulté / Réclamation**: unforeseen external disruption / anything hindering delivery / a formal written complaint — three different things requiring different handling.

## Annexe — reduced audit scope for pre-existing certified/labelled OFs

If the trainer's entity already held a certification/label valid under the pre-2019 rules (art. R.6316-3 as of 31/12/2018) and is still active, the *initial* audit can be limited to: indicateurs communs **1, 2, 11, 12, 22, 24, 25, 26, 32** + all applicable indicateurs spécifiques. Not relevant for a brand-new Odoo training activity, but worth knowing if bundled under an existing certified structure.
